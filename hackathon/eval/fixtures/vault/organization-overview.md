# Organization Overview

Acme Assist was founded in 2021.
The team has 18 full-time staff members.
The primary service region is the Pacific Northwest.
The mobile app pilot serves public libraries and school counselors.
The accessibility backlog dropped from 11 issues to 4 after the April patch.
Launch readiness depends on the vendor export fix tracked in [[risk-log]].
